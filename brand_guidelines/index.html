Four grounds, one shape, no second colour. Because the lines are true holes rather than white strokes, the mark never carries a background of its own — it sits on the paper, the panel, or a photograph equally. That is what makes it safe as a stamp, an emboss, or a favicon.

Anatomy

24 × 24 grid, six tear teeth
  • Six teeth, one rip. The top edge alternates between 5.15 and 7.05 across the full width. Six peaks is the most that stays legible at 16px — more and the tear turns into noise.
  • Flat where it was cut. The bottom is dead level at 21, because that is the edge the till cut. The contrast between a torn top and a cut bottom is what makes it read as paper rather than a shape.
  • Four lines, four margins. Each extracted row ends somewhere different — 16.8, 20.2, 13.6, 18.4. Even margins would read as a paragraph; ragged ones read as fields the system found.
  • 1.5 Rows are holes, not strokes. Each line is 1.5 units tall — a full pixel at 16px — and cut clean through the sheet, so it holds on any background without a white plate behind it.
